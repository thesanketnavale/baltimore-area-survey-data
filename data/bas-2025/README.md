2025 Baltimore Area Survey
==========================

Data for the 2025 Baltimore Area Survey may be found in this directory. Documentation of the data, including data collection methods and a codebook, are included on the [documentation page](https://jhucities.github.io/baltimore-area-survey-data/bas-2025).

--------
